We serve authentic Thai cuisine and warm hospitality to the Wilton community. Our experienced chefs will prepare and cook every dish for you to order from finest ingredients including premium spices and herbs from Thailand.

Every dish tells a story of tradition, passion, and the vibrant flavors of Southeast Asia. From our kitchen to your table, we bring you an unforgettable dining experience.
